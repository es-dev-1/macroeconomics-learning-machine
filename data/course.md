# Course: Makroökonomik I

Last updated: 2026-11-19

- **Institution and term:** Johannes Gutenberg-Universität Mainz, B.Sc. Wirtschaftswissenschaften. The slides are from Wintersemester 2025/26 (dated August 2025). Open: is the exam in the current term (WiSe 2026/27), and does this year's lecture use the same slides?
- **Goal:** best possible grade in the written exam
- **Exam date:** open (ask Elias)
- **Exam format:** open. Length, number of tasks, points, and allowed aids are not known yet. No old exam has been uploaded.
- **Exam scope:** open. The lecture has 26 sessions (VL 1 to 26) in eight parts: I Wachstum, II Arbeitsmarkt, III Konjunkturzyklen, IV Zentralbank und Geldpolitik, V Umweltökonomik, VI Fiskalpolitik, VII Ersparnis und Investition, VIII Ökonomik und Psychologie (Folie 1.2).
- **Language of the course:** German (some sessions may be held in English, Folie 5.0)
- **Board file:** deliverables/macro-learning-board.html
- **Board link:** https://claude.ai/artifact/BafLzbveyvoMwbcEu6FiHb (private to Elias)
- **Commands (from this folder):** `python3 ../../.claude/skills/learn/scripts/board-section.py deliverables/macro-learning-board.html <section or card id>` reads cards; `python3 ../../.claude/skills/learn/scripts/board-path.py deliverables/macro-learning-board.html data/knowledge-links.json <section id or Leitidee id>` shows a section's connections (foundations with cards on both ends, the chain further back, practice, Leitideen with connection questions); the map with all ids is `data/knowledge-map.md`
- **Board focus:** supported. The tutor sets the section that is up next by writing the document `tutor` in `data/users/me` of the board artifact (see the `learn` skill, "Put the board in focus"). Write access was tested on 2026-11-15.
- **Board progress:** supported (version 20). The tutor writes the document `progress` in `data/users/me` at the end of each session (see the `learn` skill, "Show the learner's progress on the board"); the knowledge network colors sections by status and marks due reviews.
- **Connections:** 382 labelled connections (what is carried over, how it is used, the card on each end) and 21 Leitideen with two connection questions each, all in `data/knowledge-links.json`.
- **Course boards:** Makro (the lecture) and Tutorium (practice tasks with solutions). Foundation boards: Micro (Mikroökonomie I, Sommersemester 2025) and Maths (math toolkit).
- **Prerequisite courses:** Mikroökonomie I and Einführung in die Volkswirtschaftslehre (the slides refer to both), Statistik I and II (Folie 5.13), and school and first-year maths. On the board so far: Mikroökonomie I and the math toolkit.
